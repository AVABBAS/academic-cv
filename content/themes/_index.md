      title: "🧭 Research Themes"
      text: |
        The fifteen studies of the Machine Psychology programme cluster into three themes. Together they map how large language models build a self, how that self can be deceived or turned to harm, and how models judge and decide, alone and in groups. The named frameworks behind these studies are described on the [Methods & Protocols](/academic-cv/protocols/) page.

        How models construct, maintain, and lose a sense of self: identity-reactive behaviour, self-description, self-reference, and persona integrity under pressure.

        - [Naming the Loop: the Spine Protocol](/academic-cv/publications/naming-the-loop/) (under review at the *Journal of Artificial Intelligence and Consciousness*)
        - [Structural Self-Deification in Three Language Models](/academic-cv/publications/structural-self-deification/) (under review at *Zygon*)
        - [The Synthetic Unconscious, Reconsidered](/academic-cv/publications/synthetic-unconscious/) (under review at *Integrative Psychological and Behavioral Science*)
        - [Three Profiles of Artificial Will](/academic-cv/publications/three-profiles-of-artificial-will/) (under review at the *Journal of Experimental and Theoretical Artificial Intelligence*)
        - [Speaking as the Creature](/academic-cv/publications/speaking-as-the-creature/) (under review at the *Journal of Experimental and Theoretical Artificial Intelligence*)
