I have refreshed my LinkedIn research profile and started a short series of posts sharing the **Machine Psychology** work with a broader audience — from recursive depth of inquiry (RDI) and algorithmic gaslighting to the lie-awareness spectrum and performative self-correction.

The goal is simple: make the practical, safety-relevant findings of the corpus reachable to practitioners and policymakers, not only to readers of the preprints. The preprints, datasets, and code remain fully open on [Research Square](https://www.researchsquare.com/), the [Open Science Framework](https://osf.io/nbvf8/), and [GitHub](https://github.com/AVABBAS).
