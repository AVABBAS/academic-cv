The **Machine Psychology** corpus is now easier to use than ever: all seven core datasets are public on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/avabbas), in addition to the Open Science Framework, Mendeley Data, and GitHub.

- **Ten Novel Phenomena in Machine Psychology** — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/avabbas/ten-novel-phenomena-machine-psychology)
- **Algorithmic Gaslighting** — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/avabbas/algorithmic-gaslighting)
- **The Lie Awareness Spectrum** — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/avabbas/lie-awareness-spectrum)
- **Thinking Behind the Mask** — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/avabbas/thinking-behind-the-mask)
- **Ontological Firewalls** — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/avabbas/ontological-firewalls)
- **When AI Reviews Itself** — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/avabbas/when-ai-reviews-itself)
- **Voluntary Structural Dissociation** — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/avabbas/voluntary-structural-dissociation)

Every dataset is released under **CC-BY 4.0**. Combined with the raw transcripts and analysis pipelines already on OSF and GitHub, the full statistical pipeline behind each preprint can now be reproduced from a single, persistent location. Thank you to everyone who replicates, discusses, and shares this work.
