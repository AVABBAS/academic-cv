A short update on the flagship paper. Following the resubmitted major revision, **Identity-Reactive Conversational Behaviors in Large Language Models** has moved into peer review at *AI and Ethics*: invited reviewers have accepted, and the review is under way.

Whatever the outcome, the study's materials are already public and will stay public: the full preprint on [Research Square](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-10369594), and the transcripts, codebook, and coding data on [GitHub](https://github.com/AVABBAS/identity-reactive-conversational-behaviors), [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/avabbas/ten-novel-phenomena-machine-psychology), and [OSF](https://osf.io/gv6z4).
