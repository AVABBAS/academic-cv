The last of the fifteen works now has a public preprint. *Three Profiles of Artificial Will*, with Mahsa Borhani, is posted on Zenodo: [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24013229](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24013229).

Every work in the Machine Psychology corpus is now public as a preprint, twelve on Research Square and three on Zenodo, and all fifteen are under review. *Three Profiles of Artificial Will* has been submitted to the *Journal of Experimental and Theoretical Artificial Intelligence*, where *Speaking as the Creature* is also under review following its resubmission this week.
