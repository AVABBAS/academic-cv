Three further preprints from the Machine Psychology programme were posted on Research Square today:

- *Adding a Neutral Third Agent to LLM Deliberation* (under review at Ethics and Information Technology): [DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-11612929/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11612929/v1)
- *Speaking as the Creature* (under review at the Journal of Experimental & Theoretical Artificial Intelligence): [DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-11613008/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11613008/v1)
- *The Synthetic Unconscious, Reconsidered*, with Mahsa Borhani (under review at Integrative Psychological and Behavioral Science): [DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-11625507/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11625507/v1)

Fourteen of the programme's fifteen works are now public as preprints (twelve on Research Square, two on Zenodo). The remaining work, *Three Profiles of Artificial Will*, is in prescreening at Research Square.
