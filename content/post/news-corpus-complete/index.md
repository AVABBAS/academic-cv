The research programme reached a milestone this week. All fifteen studies in the corpus are now under review: fourteen at international journals, including *AI and Ethics*, *AI and Society*, *Minds and Machines*, *Ethics and Information Technology*, and *Security Journal*, and one at the CALE-AI workshop.

Eleven preprints are public on Research Square and Zenodo. For every study, the complete materials (transcripts, protocols, codebooks, coding data, and analysis code) are openly deposited in a dedicated [GitHub repository](https://github.com/AVABBAS) and a matching [Hugging Face dataset](https://huggingface.co/avabbas), with archival deposits on the Open Science Framework. The full publications list on this site now covers all fifteen works with their journal status.

**Highlights**

- [Publications](/academic-cv/publications/): all fifteen works with their journal status
- [GitHub](https://github.com/AVABBAS): fifteen study repositories with open materials
