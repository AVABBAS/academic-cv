The open-science layer of the programme is finished. For each of the fifteen studies there is now:

- A dedicated [GitHub repository](https://github.com/AVABBAS) with the transcripts, protocols, codebooks, coding data, and analysis code, and an honest note on what was not retained
- A matching [Hugging Face dataset](https://huggingface.co/avabbas) with the same curated materials
- An archival deposit on the Open Science Framework

Four Hugging Face collections organise the corpus, from the complete set, [Machine Psychology: Complete Study Materials](https://huggingface.co/collections/avabbas/machine-psychology-complete-study-materials-6aeb7ed0fbd87ba49c14bff9), to thematic shelves on identity, safety, and judgment. A [showcase space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/avabbas/machine-psychology) presents all fifteen studies on one page.

Two further preprints joined Zenodo this week: Naming the Loop ([DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23955704](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23955704)) and Structural Self-Deification ([DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23955786](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23955786)). Eleven preprints are now public, and the remaining four are in prescreening at Research Square.

No manuscript text lives in any repository or dataset, by design. What is open is everything a reader needs to check the work.
