Research is not the only writing leaving this desk. **And We Will Dance One Day**, a memoir of about 80,000 words written in Persian and translated into English, is now under consideration in a full submission round.

The full manuscript is with **Dzanc Books** and **Split/Lip Press**. Excerpts are under consideration at **AGNI**, **The Common**, **ACM Translations**, **Four Way Review**, and **Markaz**.

The book tells the story of a childhood divided by divorce and war, a father's return to Iraq and the choice between his two families, and a mother who held a household together through all of it. It is, in its own way, also a study of minds under pressure, which is perhaps why it sits comfortably next to the research programme on this site.
