Two updates on the **Machine Psychology** corpus (seven preprints, all published on Research Square in July 2026 under CC-BY 4.0):

## 1. Full open-data coverage

All core datasets, raw transcripts, codebooks, and analysis pipelines are now public on the [Open Science Framework](https://osf.io/nbvf8/):

- **Ten Novel Phenomena in Machine Psychology** — [OSF](https://osf.io/gv6z4) and [Mendeley Data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/h76chwbx83) (DOI: [10.17632/h76chwbx83.1](https://doi.org/10.17632/h76chwbx83.1))
- **Algorithmic Gaslighting** (RDI coding rubric, blind codebook, 15 multi-turn transcripts) — [OSF](https://osf.io/qsvty) + [code](https://github.com/AVABBAS/algorithmic-gaslighting)
- **The Lie Awareness Spectrum** (L0–L5 protocols) — [OSF](https://osf.io/29nc6) + [code](https://github.com/AVABBAS/lie-awareness-spectrum)
- **Thinking Behind the Mask** (15 experiments, 199+ item governance library) — [OSF](https://osf.io/264qc)
- **Ontological Firewalls** (OAPD protocol, 24 experimental runs) — [OSF](https://osf.io/2d5zy)

Anyone can now replicate the statistical results end to end.

## 2. Growing international coverage and indexing

Since July, the corpus has been independently summarized, reviewed, and indexed, including:

- A full explainer on **gist.science** ([link](https://gist.science/paper/rs/rs-10386085))
- A critical review in the **Synthetic Personality Review**
- Multiple entries on **ecoruspace.me**, a Russian-language science-news aggregator
- Indexing in the **SwarmLabs / GeneTech** Chinese AI Safety & Alignment knowledge engine ([data.swarmlabs.tools](https://data.swarmlabs.tools/ai-safety/)) under AI safety and alignment keywords
- All seven preprints indexed on **alphaXiv**, **DeepDyve**, **Sciety**, **scite.ai**, and **ResearchGate**

Thank you to everyone who has read, discussed, and shared this work. The next milestone I am most excited about: the revised manuscript of *Ten Novel Phenomena in Machine Psychology*, now submitted to **AI and Ethics** (Springer Nature).
