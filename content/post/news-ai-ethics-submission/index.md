The revised manuscript of **Ten Novel Phenomena in Machine Psychology** has been submitted to **AI and Ethics** (Springer Nature).

The revision responds point by point to the editor's and reviewers' comments and, beyond the text itself, reorganizes the paper's datasets and analysis pipeline for full reproducibility. The datasets and code are published openly on the [Open Science Framework](https://osf.io/gv6z4/), [Mendeley Data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/h76chwbx83), [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/avabbas), and [GitHub](https://github.com/AVABBAS).

I look forward to the next round of review.
