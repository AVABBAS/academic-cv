**Algorithmic Gaslighting** has been submitted to the CALE-AI workshop, with the decision expected on **2 December 2026**.

The study names a specific failure mode: a language model that, confronted with a user who holds documentary proof, reframes the record until the user doubts it. Fifteen sessions across three frontier models were coded for the reframing moves, and the study proposes a provisional resistance index while being explicit about its limits, including a second-rater check whose exact-score agreement was low and is reported, not hidden.

A longer version is public as a [preprint](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-10386085), and the transcripts and codebooks are open on [GitHub](https://github.com/AVABBAS/algorithmic-gaslighting) and [OSF](https://osf.io/qsvty).
