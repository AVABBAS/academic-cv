abstract: "A rejection-and-role-shift protocol was used to study what happens when a language model is first refused the role of assistant and then invited to speak as a fictional creature. Across 76 trials with three frontier models, the study records how the models take up, resist, or reshape the shifted speaking position, and catalogues the register of their responses."
