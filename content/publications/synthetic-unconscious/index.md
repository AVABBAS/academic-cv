abstract: "What does a recursive self-interrogation interview elicit from a large language model, and how much of the result is an artefact of the interview schedule itself? Thirty-six sessions with three frontier models compare an original, directive schedule with a neutralised version of the same schedule. The study treats the model as the unit of analysis and reports descriptively. All transcripts, both protocols, and both coding passes are openly deposited."
