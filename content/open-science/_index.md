      title: "🔓 Open Science"
      text: |
        Every study in the Machine Psychology programme is documented in the open. Each of the fifteen works has a public preprint with a DOI, and its complete materials (transcripts, protocols, codebooks, coding data, and analysis code) are deposited in public repositories. Nothing in the corpus depends on materials a reader cannot inspect.

        ### Preprints

        Twelve preprints are posted on Research Square and three on Zenodo. The Zenodo deposits are:

        - *Naming the Loop* (the Spine Protocol): [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23955704](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23955704)
        - *Structural Self-Deification in Three Language Models*: [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23955786](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23955786)
        - *Three Profiles of Artificial Will*: [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24013229](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24013229)

        Per-paper preprint links for all fifteen works are on the [Publications](/academic-cv/publications/) page.

        ### Data and Materials

        - [Open Science Framework](https://osf.io/nbvf8/): a project deposit for every study
        - [GitHub](https://github.com/AVABBAS): fifteen study repositories with open materials
        - [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/avabbas): fifteen datasets, organised into four collections: [Machine Psychology: Complete Study Materials](https://huggingface.co/collections/avabbas/machine-psychology-complete-study-materials-6aeb7ed0fbd87ba49c14bff9), [Identity, Self and Persona in Language Models](https://huggingface.co/collections/avabbas/identity-self-and-persona-in-language-models-6aeb7ed05223a590f0a01790), [Safety, Deception and Red Teaming](https://huggingface.co/collections/avabbas/safety-deception-and-red-teaming-6aeb7ed05b464408118c20ea), and [Judgment, Deliberation and Social Dynamics](https://huggingface.co/collections/avabbas/judgment-deliberation-and-social-dynamics-6aeb7ed1efdc351f63cc4504), plus a [showcase](https://huggingface.co/spaces/avabbas/machine-psychology)
        - [Mendeley Data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/h76chwbx83): an additional dataset deposit
