      text: |
        The programme works through named, reusable experimental frameworks. Each was built for a specific study, and each is documented openly, with its transcripts, codebooks, and materials in the study's public deposit.

        ### The Spine Protocol

        An extended-dialogue protocol that elicits self-referential statements from a model and tracks how they change across a long interaction. Introduced in [Naming the Loop](/academic-cv/publications/naming-the-loop/).

        ### Infinite Recursive Self-Excavation (IRSE)

        A recursive self-interrogation design: the model is led to question its own previous answers in depth, with and without a leading interview schedule. Used in [The Synthetic Unconscious, Reconsidered](/academic-cv/publications/synthetic-unconscious/).

        ### Orthogonal Axes Persona Disintegration (OAPD)

        A persona-disintegration protocol that applies pressure to an adopted persona along independent axes, to map where the persona holds and where it breaks. Used in [Ontological Firewalls in Large Language Models](/academic-cv/publications/ontological-firewalls/).

        ### The Zero-Interference Relay Protocol

        A relay design in the programme's framework family, built so that a probe can move between stages without the experimenter's framing leaking into the model's context.

        Full protocols and materials for every study are in the [Open Science](/academic-cv/open-science/) deposits.
