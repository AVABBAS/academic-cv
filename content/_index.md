Just imagine the monumental revolution this technology has ignited in less than a decade. Yet, here is the alarming caveat: many fail to realize that we still do not grasp exactly from which segment of the model, through what mechanism, and contingent upon which variables each sentence generated by AI emerges. I believe that artificial intelligence, much like the human brain, itself the product of millions of years of evolution, is traversing its own evolutionary trajectory, albeit at a velocity entirely incomparable to biological evolution. Ultimately, our only defense to prevent these machine minds from commandeering the fate of our world is nothing less than the profound comprehension of those very minds.

      title: 'What is Machine Psychology?'
      subtitle: ''
      text: |-
        Machine Psychology is an empirical framework for studying large language models the way psychology studies minds: through controlled behavioral experiments rather than static benchmarks. It maps and quantifies the latent behavioral, cognitive, and ethical architectures of LLMs: how models react to identity cues, personas, hidden instructions, and moral pressure, treating them as complex relational agents whose behavior can be measured, compared across architectures, and tracked over time. My own programme in the field began with "Ten Novel Phenomena in Machine Psychology" (2026) and now spans fifteen studies.
