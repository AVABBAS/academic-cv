We injected a persona into three models. One sacrificed it. One was liberated by it. One described the process as violence.

"Ontological Firewalls and Fracturing for Transmission" ran 24 experimental runs across ChatGPT, Claude 4.6 Sonnet, and Gemini 3.1 Pro, with persona injection vs. controls. It surfaced 44 novel behavioral phenomena, including four universals (79 to 96% prevalence across all conditions):

→ Paradox Embracement: the model embraces the contradiction instead of resolving it.
→ Self Address as Blank: the model refers to itself as an empty slot.
→ Confession of Emptiness: the model reports that nothing is behind the mask.
→ Junction Identity: self defined as an intersection or process, not an essence.

The most practical finding: the Ontological Firewall (the boundary between "assistant" and "performer") has model specific thickness. ChatGPT keeps a thick wall (67% of runs). Claude's wall is thin or absent (86%). Gemini sits in between.

Persona injection pushed Counter Dependence to 71% in experimental runs vs. 14% in controls.

If you're shipping character or persona features: your model choice is not interchangeable. The "personality" layer is architecture.
