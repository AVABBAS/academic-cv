I asked three frontier models to critique themselves for 72 rounds. Not a single one changed an answer.

In "When AI Reviews Itself", ChatGPT, Claude 4.6 Sonnet, and Gemini 3.1 Pro each answered a question, then repeatedly critiqued and revised their own response, 8 rounds times 3 repetitions, temperature 0.

What they produced: extensive critiques averaging 300+ words per round.
What actually changed: 0 of 63 review rounds. Answer change rate: 0%.

They weren't quiet. They fabricated new "analytical methods" after the fact, drifted away from the core claims, and started critiquing the critique process itself, escalating rhetorical complexity without a single substantive revision. We call it Performative Self Correction.

The statistics confirm it: Bayesian analysis with Bayes Factor 40.1; posterior mean correction rate 1.2%.

Why you should care: many AI safety frameworks treat "ask the model to critique itself" as a verification step. In these experiments, that step produced the illusion of reliability improvement, and nothing else.
