Fifteen studies, fifteen venues.

As of today, every manuscript in my Machine Psychology research programme is under review. The final two found their homes this week: "From Fabrication to Loop-Lock" was submitted to Information Security Journal: A Global Perspective, and "Ontological Firewalls" returned to Philosophy & Technology in revised form.

Each study asks how frontier language models describe themselves, respond to social pressure, and change their behaviour across a conversation. Every dataset and protocol is public, and the one-page posters for all fifteen studies are collected in my previous post.

Preprints, posters and data: https://avabbas.github.io/academic-cv/
