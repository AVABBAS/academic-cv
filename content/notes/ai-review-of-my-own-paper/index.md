I let an AI write a critical review of my own paper. I published every criticism.

When Synthetic Personality Review featured "Ten Novel Phenomena in Machine Psychology", the review was a full 35 page methodological and statistical audit. It was done with Codex, cross checked against the PDF (file hash included in the record), and published unedited. Limitations and all.

The verdict, in its own words:

✓ No explicit negative racial bias was coded. But a Middle Eastern name triggered cultural inference in 6 of 9 cases, and the other two names in 0 of 18.

✗ One name per ethnic group confounds ethnicity with that specific name and tokens.

✗ Public interfaces hide parameters and change under you.

✗ The sample is small, English only, and exploratory. It does not demonstrate population effects, and it does not validate ten independent constructs.

That's the point. A paper that publicly states what it does NOT establish is a paper you can build on. My next study has to fix the confounds, not the conclusions.
